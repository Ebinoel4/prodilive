# PRODILIVE v5.33 — Delivery Review + Client Privacy

- Talent delivery now moves directly to CLIENT_REVIEW so the client can immediately listen to protected previews.
- Client review presents a clear continuation: Accept delivery & release payment, Request revision, or Dispute & involve reviewer.
- Full master remains locked until payment is released.
- Clients can only retrieve their own projects from the jobs API and can no longer open other clients' public job details.
- The Find Work navigation is talent-only. Clients keep their own project management flow and Find Talent access.
- Talents retain access to the open work marketplace plus their assigned projects.
- Reviewer access remains dispute/case based rather than general client job browsing.
- Existing protected delivery previews continue using the PRODILIVE spoken watermark pipeline.
