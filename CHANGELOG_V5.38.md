# PRODILIVE v5.38
- Platform Wallet for deposit fees, job commissions and marketplace commissions.
- Admin-configurable deposit, job and marketplace fee percentages.
- Admin revenue withdrawal ledger.
- Job posters can set a delivery deadline, optional auto-cancel and overdue grace period.
- Auto-cancel only occurs when enabled and no delivery was submitted; funded payments are refunded.
- Disputes now have a self-settlement window and reviewer resolution SLA.
- Before reviewer intervention, the job poster can accept delivery and release payment, automatically closing the dispute as resolved by the parties.
- Reviewer claiming/intervention waits for the settlement window unless Admin intervenes.
